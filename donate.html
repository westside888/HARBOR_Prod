Help veteran aviation mechanics overcome the barriers between years of military experience and rewarding civilian aviation careers.

Veteran aviation mechanics have spent years keeping some of the world's most sophisticated aircraft flying. They have the skills, discipline, and experience our civilian aviation industry urgently needs.

But when they leave military service, gaps in guidance, certification, funding, and employer connections can stand between their military experience and the civilian careers they're ready to begin.

Warriors In Need (WIN) bridges that gap.

An Untapped Resource. An Urgent Need.

America Has Already Invested

America's civilian aviation industry needs skilled technicians. At the same time, highly trained military aviation mechanics are leaving active duty with years of experience and looking for their next mission.

As WIN Founder Ben Ingram says in our short documentary, "Our entire veteran community is an incredible, untapped resource, and we can solve massive problems if we tap into that resource that taxpayers' dollars have funded. Every one of us has invested."

America has already invested in developing their skills. WIN helps ensure that investment continues to benefit our veterans, our aviation industry, and our nation.

The Path Forward

WIN Bridges The Gap From Military Service To A Rewarding Civilian Career

Veteran aviation mechanics leave military service with years of experience, but translating that experience into a civilian aviation career isn't always straightforward.

WIN helps veterans navigate the path forward, from mentoring and FAA certification preparation to financial assistance, professional tools, and connections with employers who value their skills.

We also fill a critical funding gap. While the VA and other organizations primarily fund traditional Part 147 aviation schools, many experienced veterans only need shorter Part 65 FAA preparation. That training, along with lodging, testing costs, and the professional tools needed to begin work, is not consistently covered.

Our Conviction

We Built The Bridge So Veterans Can Cross

WIN was born from a simple conviction: when you've made it through a difficult transition, you have a responsibility to help those coming behind you.

We've lived the uncertainty and learned where the barriers exist. So we created the guidance, resources, financial support, and employer connections we wish we'd had when we transitioned from military service.

Through our Evaluate → Prepare → Invest → Connect model, WIN helps veterans understand their opportunities, prepare for FAA certification, overcome financial barriers, and connect with aviation employers who need their skills.

We don't rescue veterans. We equip them to take the skills they've already earned into their next mission.
